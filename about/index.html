About Aerocotton

A family, a town,
and one fibre.

Established in 2010, Aerocotton is a family-run manufacturer and exporter of premium home textile products based in Karur, Tamil Nadu, India.

Our story

Started for the craft. Kept for the people.

The mill began with a small set of looms and a conviction that cotton, woven honestly, sells itself. The first years were spent supplying Indian homes; by 2015 the same cloth was crossing oceans — and holding its own in markets that test every seam.

Growth was deliberately slow. Capacity was added only when quality could be held; clients were added only when delivery promises could be kept without excuses. Fifteen years on, the business is measured less by its machinery count than by how many buyers return each season.

Our values

Four rules the looms answer to.

01

Material honesty

We specify what the cloth is and let it perform. No embellishment of fibre, count, or origin — the product must survive its label being checked.

02

Quiet precision

Measurements, tolerances, finishing. Craft shows itself most clearly in the details a customer never has to think about.

03

Long relationships

Family-run means we plan in decades, not seasons. Buyers return because the eighth order matches the first.

04

Continuous care

From fibre selection to export packing, every stage is owned by someone whose name we know. Responsibility that doesn't outsource.

The facility

Where the cloth is born.

Our Karur facility pairs loom floors with in-house checking, mending and packing. Buyers are welcome to visit — seeing the cloth made has closed more orders than any brochure.

Quality commitment: every programme is inspected at weaving, dyeing and finishing stages, with specified tolerances and documentation that matches the product.
